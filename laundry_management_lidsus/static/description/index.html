Laundry Management

Full service order management for laundry operations, including order flow, delivery and dispatch guides, claims, and integrated invoicing.
Developed by LeadSolutions Cia. Ltda. for Odoo 18.

Service Orders

Complete service order registration with garment tracking by type, color, washing method, and quantity. Automatic sequence numbering per company.

  • 7-step workflow: Received → Sent → In Process → In Transit → Ready → Delivered / Claim
  • Fields locked automatically outside the Received state
  • Invoice creation directly from the order
  • Delivery address selection from customer locations
  • Form, list, kanban, pivot, and graph views

Delivery and Dispatch Guides

Automatic generation of customer delivery guides and internal dispatch guides with full traceability of garment movement.

  • Customer delivery guide with garment details
  • Internal dispatch guide with washing instructions
  • Automatic sequence numbering by series and company
  • Printable custom report templates
  • Receipt confirmation tracking

Claims Management

Track damaged, lost or defective garments with detailed claim lines linked directly to service orders.

  • Configurable claim types
  • Claim line details for each garment
  • Full claim state workflow
  • Automatic linkage with service orders

Reports and Analysis

  • Pivot views for cross analysis by customer, state and plant
  • Bar charts and trend analysis
  • Totals for invoiced amounts and processed garments
  • Excel export support

Note: This module requires Laundry Configuration as a base dependency. It is recommended to install Laundry Setup to include this module and the full laundry management experience.
